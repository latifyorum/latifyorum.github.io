ABOUTISTANBUL / REMOTE

I work at the intersection of music production and audio engineering.

My approach starts with the song rather than the plugin. I care about arrangement, performance, texture, dynamics, space, and the small decisions that give a record its identity.

My work spans production, recording, editing, mixing, mastering, and sound design. Some records need a fader move; others need the arrangement rebuilt before a single plugin is opened. The job is knowing which one you are looking at.

    METHODPRINCIPLES

    Process over presets.

    A preset is somebody else's decision about a song they never heard. The work begins with listening — to the arrangement, to the room, to what the performance is already doing — and continues through references, experiments that get thrown away, and choices made for a reason that can be stated out loud. Plugins are the last step, not the method.
